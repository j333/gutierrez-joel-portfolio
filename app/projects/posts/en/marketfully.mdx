## How do you keep AI marketing on brief?

After [GetGloby](/getgloby), I designed Marketfully.ai: the same localization and transcreation work, now in one product with content creation for global brands and agencies.

<Facts>
  <Fact title="The brief">Language, location, tone, audience, topic, objective, and keywords stayed attached to whatever came next.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The workspace">Organizations held teams, so one company could run marketing without splitting the brand across tools.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The loop">Assistants iterated the work. Expert services stepped in when a human had to take the asset.</Fact>
</Facts>

### Problem: generated work drifted off the brand.

Marketing specialists needed a brief they could shape, with AI in the loop, then turn into assets that did not drift. The design problem was keeping generated work on-brand and tied to that brief.

![A brief holding language, tone, audience, and keywords above the assets it produces](/projects/marketfully/stack.webp#wide "The brief stayed the source of truth for everything generated after it.")

### Opportunity: one product for brands and the agencies that serve them.

Brands and agencies sat in the same structure. A brief could be drafted with assistance, adjusted by specialists, and then converted into content. Image generation and editing lived in the same loop. The asset stayed bound to the brief either way.

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Shared language">Product definition and customer experience needed one language across assets, translation, and agency brands.</Fact>
  <Fact title="AI workflows">Generated marketing, translation flows, and day-to-day marketing needs had to meet in the same product.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The practice">I defined team structures, technology stacks, and product roadmaps, and partially managed development.</Fact>
</Facts>

## A design system the workflow can share

I built a design system so product definition and customer experience shared one language across AI-generated marketing assets, translation flows, and the agency brands under [Marketfully](https://marketfully.com/). UX, UI, and CX stayed connected. The interface had to serve the brand as much as the workflow.

![Design system covering AI marketing assets, translation, and agency brands](/projects/marketfully/design-system.webp#wide "One language for the product, the assets, and the brands under Marketfully.")

I built and led a design and product team (a Product Designer, an AI Solution Designer, and a UX Researcher) who reported directly to me. Together we owned how the product looked, how it behaved, and how we learned from the people using it. I also built and led an AI team to deliver AI-based solutions, working side by side with QA, engineering, and AI engineers.

![Design, product, and AI teams working beside QA and engineering](/projects/marketfully/team.webp#wide "The design and product team, and the AI team beside QA and engineering.")

## What we were willing to trade

Speed of generation was not the point. An asset that left the brief was a failed asset, even when an expert had to take it the rest of the way.

> The asset stayed bound to the brief either way.

## What I learned

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Bind the asset">Generated work stays on-brand only if the brief is still attached when someone edits it.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The practice travels">Standards, rituals, and a working relationship with engineering, started at GetGloby, are what the platform still runs on.</Fact>
</Facts>
