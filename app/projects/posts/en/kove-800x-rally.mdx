## How do you raise perceived value without a new story?

This is a personal experiment on [Kove](https://www.kovemoto.com/)’s [800X Rally](https://www.kovemoto.com/kove-800x-rally): not a commissioned case, and not a live product. Kove already builds performance motorcycles. The intent was to add to that: simplify the brand and the tank graphics, and design the first impression of the model page, so perceived value sits closer to what the bike already is.

[Zhang Xue](http://en.people.cn/n3/2026/0405/c90000-20443529.html) started as a mechanic and a motocross rider. He [founded Kove in 2017](https://barebonesmc.com/kove-the-most-exiting-new-motorcycle-brand-of-2023/) with motorsport as the destination. If he could not ride Dakar himself, he would build a bike that could. In 2023 the factory [finished Dakar with every motorcycle it entered](https://www.kovemoto.com/rally-racing), a first for a Chinese manufacturer on debut. That line (rider, engineer, race to road) is the concept. Performance. This study did not invent a new one. It tried to let that idea read more clearly on the 800X Rally.

Before changing a mark, I read Kove against the category. The line was already performance. The color was already teal. The board put the 800X Rally next to Yamaha, Honda, BMW, Kawasaki, CFMoto, and KTM, and laid out what the site was trying to say.

![Research board for Kove: brand line, teal and black palette, wordmark studies, sitemap, competitor sites for Yamaha, Honda, BMW, Kawasaki, CFMoto, and KTM, and a comparison of logo, slogan, color, and segments](/projects/kove-800x-rally/research.webp#wide "Research: the brand line, the sitemap, and how Kove’s teal sits next to the category.")

The moodboard kept one instruction: focus on performance. Wordmark studies, the teal and black palette, and references for the bike and the page.

![Moodboard titled Focus On Performance, with Kove wordmark studies, a teal and black palette, motorcycle references, and early landing frames](/projects/kove-800x-rally/moodboard.webp#wide "Direction: focus on performance, with the wordmark, the teal, and the bike.")

### Problem: the side view reads as a parts diagram.

On a motorcycle, perceived value starts on the tank. The factory side view still reads as a parts diagram: 800 on the cowl, KOVE on the panel, and extra marks competing for the same plastic.

![Kove 800X Rally factory studio side view with 800 on the cowl and KOVE on the side panel](/projects/kove-800x-rally/original.webp#wide "Factory graphics: 800 on the cowl, KOVE on the side panel.")

### Opportunity: let the performance that is already there read clearly.

The existing Kove mark is a manufacturer lockup. For this study I redrew the wordmark and how it sits on fairings, so the name could live at motorcycle scale without competing with the model code. The system stayed monochrome plus the bike’s teal. Type and spacing did the hierarchy.

<Facts>
  <Fact title="One line">KOVE, 800X, and Rally had to read as one performance line on plastic, in print, and on a screen.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Less decoration">The silhouette, the teal, and a few large letters carried the bike.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Four specs">799 cc, 180 km/h, 95 hp, 176 kg. The photograph sells. The numbers confirm.</Fact>
</Facts>

## Brand and graphics at motorcycle scale

I cleaned the livery so the silhouette, the teal, and a few large letters could carry performance without extra decoration. The adventure-bike architecture stays: tall screen, crash bars, knobby tires, with the identity on the surfaces people see first.

![Kove 800X Rally studio side view with teal and black Rally graphics](/projects/kove-800x-rally/studio.webp#wide "Studio: Rally graphics on the tank, fairing, and fork.")

<Label>In context</Label>

The cover is a photomontage. I used AI to place the redesigned graphics onto a bike in sand, then adjusted light and edges so the livery felt photographed rather than pasted. The point was not a perfect render. It was a fast way to judge whether the graphics held up in a rally context before spending more time on the landing.

## A quieter first impression

The landing is the first impression of the model page, what you see when you arrive at the 800X Rally. The live page already has a desert hero, a full nav, and a long model name with a paragraph on top of the photograph.

![Kove 800X Rally current model page: desert rider, full nav, and KOVE 800X RALLY CN4/E5+ headline](/projects/kove-800x-rally/current-site.webp#wide "Current site: desert hero, full nav, and a long model headline.")

The study strips that down to one hero: the desert bike, a quiet KOVE nav, the name, and four specs underneath. No extra chrome.

![Kove 800X Rally landing: desert hero, KOVE nav, model name, and four specs](/projects/kove-800x-rally/landing.webp#wide "Landing: desert hero, quiet nav, and four specs.")

## What we were willing to trade

This stayed an experiment. The work is a study of brand, graphics, and a landing, not a proposal Kove asked for, and not a claim that this is how the 800X Rally ships.
