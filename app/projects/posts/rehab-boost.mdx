Golf Boost Software Company sat at the overlap of sport, software, and rehabilitation. I designed the iOS apps, branding, and experience for both products: a golf training app and a rehabilitation exercise app.

This was part-time work, in projects rather than a continuous engagement. It ran in parallel with my full-time roles. I worked directly with the owner, who was based in Miami. The products were not a local service. They were golf training and rehabilitation apps, built to be used beyond that city.

## What I designed

I designed iOS apps that used body recognition to detect movements and give people feedback they could follow. User interviews shaped the interaction and usability decisions, so the interface followed how people actually trained and recovered.

I led the rebranding of Golf Boost, tightening the visual identity and how people met the product. I also built the design system and the visual and interaction foundations so both products felt cohesive as the team kept building.

The Rehab Boost work was more focused: get the rehabilitation product to a place where the interaction felt trustworthy, not just technically impressive.
