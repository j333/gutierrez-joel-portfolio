At Rehab Boost I designed an iOS app for rehabilitation exercises, using AI to give people guidance they could actually follow.

The stint was short and focused: get the product to a place where the interaction felt trustworthy, not just technically impressive.

## What I did

I designed the iOS experience around AI-based capabilities for rehab exercises. User interviews shaped the interaction and usability decisions, so the interface followed how people actually moved through a session.

I also contributed to the visual and interaction foundations of the product, so the system felt cohesive as the team kept building.
