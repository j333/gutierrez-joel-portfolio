Golf Boost Software Company sat at the overlap of sport, software, and a related rehab product. As User Experience Designer I worked on the brand and on an AI-driven iOS app that later connected to [Rehab Boost](/experience/rehab-boost).

The work ran in parallel with [Centric Digital](/experience/centric-digital): one client-services practice, and this product-side stretch in Miami.

## What I did

I led the rebranding of Golf Boost, tightening the visual identity and how people met the product. I also designed an AI-driven iOS app for Rehab Boost, so rehabilitation exercises had an interface that was easier to follow.

User interviews kept the work honest. The goal was not a prettier screen. It was an interface that matched how people actually trained and recovered.
