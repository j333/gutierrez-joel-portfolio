[VSPT Wine Group](https://www.vsptwinegroup.com/) was my first in-house marketing design role after design school. I made web and graphic materials for eight major brands, including winery labels that had to ship into more than 23 countries.

The constraint was production as much as aesthetics: compliance, dealers, and a catalog of assets that people needed to find again.

## What I did

I designed web and graphic materials to keep those brands visible across markets. Labels had to meet international standards, not just look right in a mockup.

I also managed and organized marketing assets for dealers, so the library was something they could actually use instead of a folder they avoided.
