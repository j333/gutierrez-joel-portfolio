[Viña Errázuriz](https://www.errazuriz.com/) was an independent client. I built a marketing resource manager so the marketing team could share assets with distributors and clients worldwide. The library covered assets across all of their brands.

This was part-time work, in projects rather than a continuous engagement. It ran in parallel with my full-time role at [Centric Digital](/experience/centric-digital). The dates mark the window of those projects, not an unbroken stretch.

## What I did

I designed the system marketing used to hand the right files to distributors and clients. Labels, photography, and dealer materials had to stay findable and consistent across brands and markets.

The product had to serve people who were not designers by default: marketing, distributors, and anyone who needed the right file without a scavenger hunt.
