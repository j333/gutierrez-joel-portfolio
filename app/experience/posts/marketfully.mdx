After GetGloby was acquired, the product became part of [Marketfully](https://marketfully.com/). I moved into Product Design Manager and kept leading how the product is defined, designed, and experienced.

The work sits at the intersection of design systems, AI-assisted content workflows, and the needs of global marketing teams. Marketfully.AI brings together adaptive translation, transcreation, and content creation. The design problem is making that complexity feel clear enough to use every day.

## What I led

I built a design system to give product definition and customer experience a shared language. That system had to hold up across AI-generated marketing assets, translation flows, and the agency brands that now sit under Marketfully.

I also led initiatives around AI marketing asset generation and translation, looking for where automation actually removes friction instead of adding another layer of process. UX, UI, and CX stay connected: the interface has to serve the brand as much as the workflow.

## Why it matters

This is the same product story as GetGloby, continued at a different scale. The job is still to turn ambiguity into structure, only now across a unified platform and a broader set of brands.
