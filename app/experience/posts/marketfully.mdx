I joined GetGloby as the first product hire and built the design practice and the design system. I took the platform from MVP to a product that was acquired by [MotionPoint](https://www.motionpoint.com/) / [Marketfully](https://marketfully.com/). The design diligence audit scored an A, the highest rating.

I then led a small design and AI pod of 5 at Marketfully: one product designer, one UX researcher, one AI design engineer, and two AI engineers. We worked on a marketing-content product with Key Content, including SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

## From MVP to product

GetGloby started as translation and localization services. I reported to the CTO and CPO. The work ran with teams across the U.S. and Europe. The brief was never only screens. It was to shape the product, sharpen the value proposition, and build the systems that would let a small team grow.

I redesigned the platform from an early MVP into a product teams could run. That meant a cohesive brand, a design system, and a backlog that engineering, product, and design could share.

We kept interactions intuitive, kept a culture of user feedback, and looked at new ways to make marketing assets accessible across languages. I built the product design practice from the ground up: standards, rituals, and a working relationship with engineering. That foundation supported the acquisition and the move into Marketfully, where GetGloby’s technology now lives inside Marketfully.AI.

## After the acquisition

I kept leading how the product is defined, designed, and shipped. I defined team structures, technology stacks, and product roadmaps, and partially managed development.

Marketfully.AI brings together adaptive translation, transcreation, and content creation for global marketing teams. The work sits where design systems, AI-based workflows, and day-to-day marketing needs meet.

The pod owned how the product looked, how it behaved, and how we learned from the people using it. We shipped side by side with QA and engineering.

I built a design system so product definition and customer experience shared one language across AI-generated marketing assets, translation flows, and the agency brands under Marketfully. UX, UI, and CX stayed connected. The interface had to serve the brand as much as the workflow.
