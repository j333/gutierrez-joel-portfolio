Centric Digital was a long stretch of product and interface work for enterprise and startup clients. I joined as a UI designer and left as a Senior Product Designer, working from Mendoza and later New York.

The through-line was the same: take complex, often regulated products and make them usable enough to ship — websites, apps, and brand systems for teams that already had a lot of surface area.

## Clients and work

I designed web and app projects for AIG, Kaiser Permanente, and Humana, and worked on brand identities for startups including Dear Jack and Premo. I also led the rebranding of Dummies, a system that had to hold up across a catalog with more than 200 million books in print and 2,500 titles.

A lot of the enterprise work lived in workshops and global code-a-thons, especially with AIG’s international teams. The job was as much alignment as interface: get design, engineering, and stakeholders onto one version of the product.

## What carried forward

Seven years of client work is where the later product roles were built. Design systems, research, and the habit of translating requirements for engineering all started here — then scaled at [GetGloby](/experience/getgloby) and [Marketfully](/experience/marketfully).
